Support

Questions, bug reports, or purchase problems? Email santosfjseo@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to fly — your guns fire automatically. Catch power-ups for extra weapon streams, shields, bombs and repairs. Destroy enough of the formation to earn stars: three stars means almost nothing got past you. Every mission ends with a fortress boss.

Bailing out

When your hull reaches zero the first time in a mission, your pilot parachutes onto the island below. Fight through the jungle on foot, grab medkits and coins, and reach the rescue seaplane within the time limit to return to the mission with a full hull. If the pilot does not make it, you can continue with gems or end the sortie.

Fuel

Each sortie costs one fuel. Fuel refills by one every five minutes up to ten, or you can refill immediately for 5 gems.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Aircraft, paint schemes, weapon packs and the Recruit Kit and Ace Pass are permanent unlocks — to restore them on a new device, open Settings (the gear icon) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Gems are consumable. They are saved on this device only, do not sync between devices, and cannot be restored once spent or after the app is deleted.

For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress

Stars, coins, gems, aircraft, upgrades and daily progress are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. There is no cloud sync. Game Center leaderboard scores and achievements stay with your Game Center account.

Rewarded ads

Watching a rewarded video is always optional. Nothing in the game is locked behind an ad, and every mission can be finished without watching one.
