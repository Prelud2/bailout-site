Bailout Squadron

A vertical-scrolling WW2 arcade shooter for iPhone. Drag to fly, your guns fire on their own, and every island ends with a four-engine fortress boss.

When your hull hits zero you don't just lose — you bail out. Parachute onto the island, fight through the jungle on foot, and reach the rescue seaplane to get straight back in the cockpit.

30 missions across three difficulties, six aircraft, paint schemes, secondary weapons, daily supply drops and Game Center leaderboards. Plays offline. Every mission can be finished without paying.
